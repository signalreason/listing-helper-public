# Support

For login, missing photos, billing, or your eBay connection, contact the person who
operates your installation first. Their public contact is on its privacy page.

For a reproducible software problem, open an issue in the repository where you obtained
the app. Include the app revision, browser, steps, expected result, and actual result.
Use invented example data. Maintainer responses are best effort; no response time or
hosted support service is promised.

Do not attach passwords, API keys, account IDs, connection strings, real listing data,
customer information, or photos with addresses or other private details. Check the
browser address bar and the whole screenshot before sharing. Do not paste full logs.

For a possible security defect, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a
public issue. For ordinary use, see the [reseller guide](docs/user-guide.md).
